How it works

  1. 1Select a factual claim on any regular webpage.
  2. 2Right-click and choose “Fact-check with Pino.”
  3. 3Read the analysis and open the original sources.
Pino sends only the selected claim, limited nearby text, the page title, and a URL without query parameters. It does not send the full page.
